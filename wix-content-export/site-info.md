## Home page copy

### Welcome to AP Explores!

#### Hi, I am Abhyudaya Pandey.

Just another engineer who is passionate about photography and traveling. I prefer being a traveler and not a tourist. I enjoy traveling to off beat places or the "touristy" places in off season. I don't believe in covering the famous spots, but interacting with the locals, exploring the culture, knowing the history and understanding the environment.

This site hosts the photographs that I have clicked and my travel blogs.

For any queries or suggestions, feel free to contact me through the form below or follow me on my Instagram: @apexplores
